# Store Listing Draft

## App name

AirplaneMode

## Short description

Take your music, videos, and personal Reel queue offline before takeoff.

## Full description

Flights feel shorter when the things you actually wanted to watch are already with you.

AirplaneMode brings downloaded media and personal Reel snapshots into one focused, offline-first Android app. Build a boarding playlist, save a stretch of your Reel feed before departure, then keep watching after the signal disappears.

**Your media, ready for takeoff**

- Browse supported media sources and import files from your device.
- Keep downloads and playlists in a native offline library.
- Listen in the background, change playback speed, or use fullscreen and picture-in-picture for video.

**A Reel queue that works offline**

- Sign in on Instagram's own website inside the app.
- Navigate normally to Reels and choose when capture starts and stops.
- Save ordered snapshots with covers, captions, creator details, audio attribution, and playable video when available.
- Resume near where you stopped while retaining the freedom to scroll back.
- Choose High, Efficient, Original, or Compact storage policy for each new snapshot.

**Local-first by design**

- No AirplaneMode account, ads, analytics, or developer-operated cloud.
- Separate controls for deleting Media, Reel snapshots, website cache, website data, or everything.
- Authentication remains inside the embedded website.

AirplaneMode is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram, Meta, Google, or YouTube. Availability can vary as third-party services change. Only download content you are entitled to access and retain.
